Galinum is pay-as-you-go. You pay for the users your product reaches, with no
plans, seats, base fee, or free allowance.

## The billing unit

Galinum bills **monthly active users (MAU)**. An end user becomes active in a
billing period when any of these events happens:

- You call `identify` or `track` for that user.
- That user sees a web in-app message.
- An email provider accepts a campaign email for that user.

An accepted campaign email counts even if the provider reports delivery later.
A send rejected before provider acceptance, suppressed, failed before
acceptance, or frequency-capped does not count.

Each end user counts **once per billing period per project**, across all
channels and messages. Sending ten accepted messages to one project recipient
costs the same as sending one. The organization total combines usage from all
its projects.

## Rates

Rates are graduated. Each portion of your usage is charged at its tier's rate.

| Monthly active users | Price per user |
| --- | --- |
| 1 – 10,000 | $0.02 |
| 10,001 – 50,000 | $0.012 |
| 50,001 – 250,000 | $0.008 |
| 250,001+ | $0.005 |

For example, 40,000 active users cost $560: the first 10,000 cost $200, and the
next 30,000 cost $360.

Prices are in USD and billed monthly in arrears. Tax is calculated at checkout
and added to your invoice.

## AI credits

Galinum-hosted agent sweeps are billed at **$0.01 per AI credit**. Agents you
run yourself are not billed for AI credits. The default monthly limit is 2,500
credits.
Owners and admins can change it under **Settings → Billing**, from 0 to
1,000,000 credits. The limit is a usage control, not a free allowance. Setting
it to 0 pauses hosted-agent sweeps.

Before each sweep, the hosted agent checks whether the organization has reached
its limit. A sweep that starts below the limit runs to completion, even if it
finishes above the limit. The next sweep is then skipped until the usage period
resets.

Galinum emails owners and admins when usage reaches 80% of the limit, and again
at 100%. Each alert is sent at most once per usage period, so crossing the same
threshold again in a later period alerts you again.

After each completed run, Galinum reports its finalized credits once to Stripe.
The report uses one stable identifier, so a retry cannot create a second charge
inside Stripe's duplicate window. If a response remains uncertain when that
24-hour window closes, Galinum waives the usage instead of risking a duplicate
charge. Galinum also waives usage that it cannot bill safely because of a
permanent rejection or unusable billing state.

Under **Settings → Billing**, you can review measured credits, billed credits,
reporting state, waived runs, and recent hosted-agent runs. Measured but waived
credits do not appear as billed cost.

## Payment method

You need a payment method on file before Galinum delivers anything. Add one
under **Settings → Billing**. Checkout puts a card on file and charges nothing
up front. Your first invoice covers only the usage you accrue.

Without a payment method, Galinum records `identify` and `track` data but
serves no web in-app messages, sends no campaign email, and accrues no MAU.

## Spend cap

You can set an optional spend cap under **Settings → Billing**. The cap is a
hard stop. When usage for the period reaches it, Galinum stops both channels
until the next period, or until you raise or remove the cap. The dashboard
warns you at 80% and 95%.

The cap limits future delivery, not what you already owe. Usage accrued before
the cap took effect stays payable.

## Email frequency protection

Galinum accepts at most **3 campaign emails per project recipient in any
rolling 24-hour window**, across all campaigns in that project. A send above
the limit is skipped permanently for that campaign, not delayed. It appears as
`frequency_capped` in delivery records and as `frequencyCapped` in campaign
stats and usage.

A capped send does not count toward MAU. Transactional service email, such as
account and authentication messages, uses a separate path and is exempt.

## What Galinum sends

Galinum sends **campaign email**: launches, releases, announcements, and other
product communications you choose to send. Keep your own transactional provider
for password resets, receipts, and account notices. Galinum does not send
transactional email for you.

Unsubscribes and suppressions are scoped to campaign email only. They do not
reach your transactional provider, so keep honoring opt-outs in the email you
send yourself.

Because campaign email is priced per active user rather than per message,
Galinum protects the shared sending reputation that price depends on. Sending
that harms deliverability, such as sustained hard bounces, spam complaints, or
purchased lists, can be throttled or paused. Galinum emails you when that
happens.

## What a pause looks like

A pause is graceful, never an application error:

- `GET /api/v1/messages` returns `200` with an empty `messages` array when
  `serving` is not `ok`.
- The SDK renders nothing and your product keeps working normally.
- `identify` and `track` keep recording data, but do not accrue MAU while
  serving is paused.
- Campaign email stops when `emailServing` is `paused`.

During payment retries, `serving` stays `ok` so web in-app delivery continues,
but `emailServing` becomes `paused`. A canceled or unpaid organization stops
both channels.

Resuming is self-serve. Add a payment method, raise the cap, or restore billing
in **Settings → Billing**. Delivery restarts within a minute. A user becomes
billable on the next qualifying activity after service resumes.

## Checking the state from your agent

Your agent reads current usage with `GET /api/v1/usage`. Check `serving` before
diagnosing web in-app delivery. Check `emailServing` before launching or
diagnosing campaign email.

```bash
curl https://galinum.com/api/v1/usage \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $GALINUM_SECRET_KEY"
```

```json
{
  "serving": "cap_reached",
  "emailServing": "paused",
  "billable": true,
  "period": {
    "start": "2026-08-05T00:00:00.000Z",
    "end": "2026-09-05T00:00:00.000Z",
    "boundary": "subscription"
  },
  "activeUsers": 50000,
  "frequencyCapped": 12,
  "estimatedSpendUsd": 680,
  "spendCapUsd": 600,
  "projects": [
    {
      "id": "prj_123",
      "name": "Acme",
      "activeUsers": 50000,
      "frequencyCapped": 12
    }
  ]
}
```

- `serving` is `ok`, `payment_required`, or `cap_reached`.
- `emailServing` is `ok` or `paused`. It pauses whenever `serving` is not `ok`
  and while billing is `past_due`.
- `frequencyCapped` counts capped campaign-email deliveries queued in the
  current period. The organization total is also broken down by project.
- `period.boundary` is `subscription` for a billable organization. Otherwise,
  usage display follows the UTC calendar month.
- Money fields are USD. Period timestamps are ISO-8601.

Only a human can restore billing or change the spend cap in the dashboard.

## Invoices and cancellation

**Settings → Billing** opens the Stripe Customer Portal. Use it to manage your
payment method, download invoices, and cancel. After cancellation takes effect,
Galinum reports `payment_required` and stops both delivery channels.
