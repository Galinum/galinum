Every project must verify its own sending domain before an agent can launch
email campaigns. Domain setup is a human dashboard task. The management API
cannot create or bypass this identity.

## Before you start

Choose a dedicated campaign subdomain such as `updates.example.com`. Use a
different subdomain from the one that sends your transactional email, such as
password resets and receipts. Campaign email attracts more unsubscribes and
spam complaints, so a separate subdomain keeps that reputation away from the
mail your users must receive.

You also need:

- A from name, such as `Acme`.
- A from address on that exact domain, such as
  `news@updates.example.com`.
- Your company's valid postal address for the required email footer.
- Access to the domain's DNS provider.

## Add the domain

1. Open **Settings → Email** in the Galinum dashboard.
2. Select the project from the project switcher.
3. Enter the sending domain, from name, from address, and company address.
4. Select **Add domain**.

Galinum creates the sending domain with its email provider and shows the DNS
records you must add.

If that domain already exists in the connected provider account, Galinum
adopts it instead. Adoption reuses the existing domain, its DNS records, and
its current verification status. Galinum never changes the configuration of an
adopted domain, so the tracking settings of your transactional mail stay as
they are. An already verified domain is ready for campaign launches at once.
A domain that another project already uses is refused.

## Add the DNS records

Copy every record shown in **Settings → Email** into your DNS provider.
Preserve each record's type, name, value, and priority. A new domain needs
SPF and DKIM records to send, plus tracking records that record opens and
clicks. Add all of them. The domain stays partly verified while any record is
missing, and a partly verified domain cannot launch a campaign.

Do not proxy these records if your DNS provider offers that option. A proxied
record fails verification.

DNS propagation can take time.

Return to **Settings → Email** and select **Verify DNS**. The status changes
to `verified` after the provider finds all required records.

## Launch requirements

An email campaign launch fails with an actionable `409` response unless:

- The project has a configured sending domain.
- The domain status is `verified`.
- The from address uses that exact verified domain.

Draft creation does not require verification. This lets your agent prepare a
campaign while a human finishes DNS setup.

## Recipient and unsubscribe behavior

Galinum reads the recipient from the end user's `email` trait. Users without
a valid email are ineligible. An unsubscribe, permanent bounce, or spam
complaint suppresses that address across all campaign email in the project.

Every email includes a signed unsubscribe link and one-click unsubscribe
headers.

## Frequency protection and billing

Galinum accepts at most **3 campaign emails per project recipient in any
rolling 24-hour window**, across every campaign in that project. A send above
the limit is skipped permanently for that campaign and recorded as
`frequency_capped`. It appears in campaign and variant stats, delivery records,
and current-period usage.

An accepted campaign email makes that recipient active for MAU billing. The
recipient counts once per billing period in that project, however many accepted
messages they receive. Sends rejected before provider acceptance, suppressed,
failed before acceptance, or frequency-capped do not accrue MAU. Transactional
service email is exempt from the frequency limit.

Before launching email, check `emailServing` with `GET /api/v1/usage`. A
`paused` value means campaign email will not send. See
[Usage & billing](/usage-billing) for payment, dunning, spend-cap, and usage
semantics.

The dashboard shows sent, frequency-capped, delivered, opened, clicked,
bounced, complaint, unsubscribe, and conversion counts on the campaign.
