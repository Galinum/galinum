Galinum is **self-driving product communications**. When you ship a feature,
launch a product, or run a promotion, you don't open a campaign builder — you
tell your own AI agent (Claude Code today) what you want to communicate, and
the agent operates Galinum for you:

- it writes the copy,
- expresses the audience as trait/event targeting against your real user data,
- launches the campaign through web in-app or email — immediately or on a
  schedule,
- and reports the channel-specific delivery and engagement results.

Most communications are exactly that simple: one message, delivered to each
eligible user at most once, measured through engagement. When you want more,
the same campaign model optionally measures a target outcome or runs
goal-driven A/B optimization — but only when you ask for it.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your product">
    Integrate the [React SDK](/sdk/react/overview) (or call the
    [HTTP API](/api/overview) directly). Identify your users, track the
    behavioral events that matter, include an email trait for email delivery,
    and mount the in-app message surface when you use that channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give your agent the keys">
    Install the [galinum skill](/skill/claude-code) in your repo and give your
    agent the project's secret key. The skill teaches it the full
    [management API](/api/overview): reading users and events, creating and
    launching campaigns, and logging every decision it makes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell it what to communicate">
    "Announce CSV export to free users who haven't tried it." The agent writes
    the message, targets the audience, launches, and reports engagement. Ask
    for more — "and tell me whether people start using it", or "A/B test this
    against the activation goal" — and it measures outcomes or optimizes
    variants instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Galinum delivers

Galinum delivers targeted **web in-app messages** and **email campaigns**.
Web in-app supports a compact toast or a centered announcement modal, with an
optional image or GIF. Email uses a verified project domain, a Markdown body,
and built-in unsubscribe and recipient-frequency protection. Each user receives
each campaign at most once, and Galinum measures the relevant lifecycle per
variant. Mobile push can later plug into the same channel abstraction.

You stay in control: the dashboard shows every campaign, delivery, and
agent-run — what your agent did and why — and campaigns can be paused or ended
at any time.
